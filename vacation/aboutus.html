About Us

Sakura Travel creates meaningful journeys through Japan that go beyond typical tourism. We combine iconic destinations like Kyoto, Tokyo, and Osaka with hidden gems to give you both a local and cultural experience. Our trips are flexible and tailored to you—whether you prefer guided tours or exploring on your own. From tea ceremonies and hot springs to food tours and unique regional discoveries, every itinerary is designed to match your interests and the season. We don’t aim for a perfect trip—we create experiences you’ll truly remember. Let us plan the journey. You make the memories.
